How to draw it. Each pose is one dot and four polylines (arms, body, two legs), round caps and joins. Stroke the pose twice: first in gamboge #e0a526 at width 7.6, then in ink #171513 at width 5 (viewBox units). That leaves a 1.3-unit rim, about 1 px at play scale (×0.68). The sash (width 3.4) and its two tails (1.4) are vermilion #cc3524 in both passes.
