Design spec · v0.1 · exploratory prototype

Firefly Relay ✦
萤火接力

A one-button co-op game for the living room. One screen is the night sky; every phone (or laptop) is a lantern. A single spark travels from lantern to lantern — tap when yours lights up, and the whole family keeps the chain alive.

2–8 players, mixed agesPC + phones togetherHome Wi-Fi only Zero install · zero internet60-second rounds
00

The 30-second pitch

Think Spaceteam's "everyone shouts together" energy crossed with the one-tap simplicity of a rhythm game, but with no losers, no reading, and no downloads. One shared object — the spark — belongs to the whole family. Nobody can win alone, nobody gets eliminated, and every 60-second round leaves a permanent star in your family's sky. That last part is the hook: "just one more round."

02

The core loop

~1.4 s per pass at level 1, tightening to ~0.5 s at level 8.
01 · SPARK LANDSOne lantern lights

Server picks a target and opens a timing window. The whole room can see whose turn it is.

→
02 · TAPOne button, one tap

That player taps before the ring closes. Everyone else watches and cheers.

→
03 · CHAINCombo grows, line drawn

Score = base × chain (capped ×8). A new glowing line joins the constellation.

→
04 · ESCALATEEvery 8 passes, faster

Window shrinks ~105 ms per level; every 9th pass is a HARMONY event.

→
05 · BANK60 s → a star

Round ends, a star is saved to the family sky, "Play again" is one tap away.

Failure is gentle. A missed tap resets the chain, dims the shared glow slightly and quietly widens that player's personal timing window. No elimination, no "you lost", no blame. The spark simply comes back around.

Research → design decisions

The idea is built from what already works, not from a hunch.

Phones-as-controllers + a room code is the proven zero-friction entry to local multiplayer; no app download, no hardware people don't already own.Jackbox "How to Play", AirConsole "About"The host PC serves the controller page over HTTP and shows a QR code. Phones join by scanning or typing a short LAN URL. A laptop can join as a lantern too.

Positive interdependence beats competition for shared attitudes and prosocial behaviour, especially with children.Frontiers, cooperative vs competitive exposure, ACM, social interaction in It Takes Two / Overcooked! 2No PvP, no leaderboard, no elimination. Round-end cards show only passes made, never misses. Shared glow meter is collective.

04

Rules in one screen

GoalKeep the spark moving. Every successful pass grows the chain and draws a line in the sky.
InputTap your big button (or press Space on a PC) when your lantern glows. That's the entire control scheme.
MissChain resets, the shared glow dips, your timing window quietly gets a little easier. The spark comes back — nobody is out.
HARMONYEvery 9th pass, all lanterns light at once. Majority tap together = double points and a big cheer.
EscalationEvery 8 passes the window tightens. Level 8 is roughly three times faster than level 1.
Between roundsOne star is added to the family sky, saved on the host. The host presses Play again; everyone keeps their phone.
05

Network architecture

📺 Host PC / TVOpens /?board=1. Shows the sky, QR, HUD, constellation. Runs no game logic of its own.
HTTP + WS
↑↓
⚙️ Node serverSingle process on 0.0.0.0:3000. Authoritative turns, timing, scoring, bots, persistent sky. Zero dependencies.
HTTP + WS
↑↓
📱 Phones / laptopsOpen the LAN URL. Each becomes one lantern with one button. Works offline, on any modern browser.

Clients are thin renderers. A small NTP-style ping/pong handshake aligns client and server clocks so a tap's timestamp can be corrected for roughly half the uplink latency — important when the family router is busy. All timing decisions are made on the server, so a slow phone never gets an unfair advantage.

06

Roadmap (if this becomes a real product)

Chaos ModeThe pass order is hidden from the board and only revealed on phones — full Spaceteam-style information asymmetry and shouting.
More event typesHold-to-charge the spark, pass-left/pass-right, golden sparks worth 5×, and a "shadow" round where the constellation goes dark.
Family sky unlocksStars unlock new constellations, lantern skins and ambient soundscapes — cosmetic only, never pay-to-win.
mDNS / .localAdvertise firefly.local so nobody has to type an IP address at all.
Spectator + replayA "watch the constellation redraw" 10-second recap at round end — shareable, and a natural cliffhanger.
AccessibilityColour-blind-safe palettes, adjustable base window, audio-only cue mode, larger tap targets for motor needs.
