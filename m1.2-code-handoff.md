### Fix 5 — Enclose indicator: categorically distinct grammar (supersedes relying on Fix 4 alone)

**Root cause, not just a missing cue:** two native-Chinese testers who fully understood 囗's meaning still never checked the center box. The 4-zone picker (top/bottom/left/right) varies along one continuous dimension — direction. Enclosure is not a 5th direction, it's a different *kind* of relationship (containment vs. adjacency). A player's mental model built from 4 visible directional options has no slot for "or something structurally different in the middle" — this is the **N+1 categorical-slot problem** (named finding, R4-D039 revision): any picker presenting N same-kind options plus one different-in-kind option will have the +1 systematically missed unless that option signals its different kind, not just its existence.

| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| Change | The center-zone indicator, when carried matter is enclosure-eligible, must use a visual grammar categorically different from the seam lines used for the 4 directional zones — not a smaller box in the same stroke style. Recommend: a soft pulsing ring or radial glow at the cell center |
| Timing | Visible from **drag-start**, not gated behind hovering the center — its presence must signal "a fifth, different-kind option exists" before the pointer approaches it |

**Self-test:** CDP — confirm the center indicator's render call is a visually distinct primitive/animation type from `drawZoneOverlay`'s seam-line rendering, and that it is present immediately on drag-start for enclosure-eligible carried matter.

## Fix 6 — Differentiated Failure Doctrine (R4-D040, `[LOCKED]`)

**Finding:** both testers' first probe was 火+水 (the single most obvious pair to try) and both received the identical generic spark. A failure response uniform across all failures carries exactly one bit of information ("wrong") regardless of how it's animated — this is a correctness verdict in costume, and it's why both testers stopped probing after ~5 attempts. §4.4's "never nothing" was satisfied by the letter, not the spirit.

| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| New default rule | An unstable composition renders the **tag reaction** between carried and host (existing reaction table, §4.5) as its fizzle content in place, then collapses. Examples: 火 onto 水 → steam burst (reaction 2); 水 onto 火(ignited) → extinguish hiss (reaction 3); luminous carried near dark-adjacent → flicker; living host + hot-adjacent → the union itself visibly flinches before collapsing |
| Existing 4 transient variants (§2.2: under-bake-土, under-generic, mud, sink-本) | Become **special cases** of this rule, unchanged in their own behavior — they already are differentiated, this rule generalizes what they were doing piecemeal |
| Fallback | Only a pair with **zero tag collision** gets the old generic spark |
| Zero new content | This reuses the existing reaction table (§4.5) — no new authored pairs required |

**Self-tests:**
- **T-F1:** 火 dragged onto standalone 水 renders the steam reaction (not the generic spark)
- **T-F2:** a genuinely tag-inert pair (confirm one exists in the M1 set, e.g. 目 onto 人) still gets the generic spark — the fallback path must still exist and fire correctly

## Fix 7 — Regrowth, narrowed scope (R4-D041 revised, `[LOCKED]`)

**Do not over-apply this.** First-principles check: Law 2's only true sink is ignition-to-ash. Walking the full M1 component table, **木 is the only primitive that is flammable** — every other component (水 土 口 鸟 人 目 上 下 火 日 月) survives indefinitely through harvest/compose/decompose regardless of whether its source well ever regrows, because none of those paths delete matter. The testers' specific complaint was "木 is burned out" — nothing else. A blanket regrowth ruling would have been solving problems that don't structurally exist.

| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| **In scope** | **Tree stand only.** Regrows on a slow diegetic timer (recommend: similar cadence to hearth's existing respawn pattern, tune in playtest) |
| **Explicitly out of scope — do not touch** | Pond, hearth, scattered singles (土口鸟人目上下), sky-slot (日月). Their matter was never at risk. Sky-slot in particular: "you can take the sun down. Once." is correct as originally written — not a scarcity choice under review, but an accurate description, since 明 is always strike-able back to 日+月 and the matter never leaves circulation even though the *cell* only empties once |

**Self-tests:**
- **T-G1a (positive):** tree-stand cell respawns 木 after its timer elapses
- **T-G1b (negative — explicitly required):** confirm pond, hearth, scattered singles, and sky-slot do **NOT** respawn after equivalent wait time. This negative test exists specifically so a future agent can't accidentally over-apply regrowth again — don't skip it because it seems redundant
