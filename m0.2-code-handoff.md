**H1 — chip circles occlude the target cell's top band (prime suspect).**
Chips are world-anchored above the avatar (your own R4-D018 note: the middle chip sits ~2px from the cell boundary above, with a 15px hit radius). To compose, the avatar must be orthogonally adjacent to the host 木. **H2 — D017 threshold + D018 bail interaction on drag start.**
The dragged chip is itself a chip. Verify that a drag *originating* on a chip still reaches the board's drop logic after D018, for releases inside vs outside another chip's circle.

**H3 — adjacency gate vs the top band geometrically.**
If the avatar stands *below* the host 木 (the natural position after walking up to it), confirm a release in the host's top band is still classified as targeting the host cell (adjacent) and not the cell above it (not adjacent → snap-back). **H4 — host state.** Confirm the 末 recipe fires on a bare 木 both (a) original in the tree stand after its neighbor is harvested and (b) re-placed from inventory onto open ground. If the recipe keys on some provenance/state flag rather than "cell contains bare 木", that's the bug.
